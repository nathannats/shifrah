About Lions Net Uganda

Lions Net Uganda was founded with a simple mission: to provide fast, reliable, and affordable internet access to everyone in Uganda. We believe that connectivity is a necessity, not a luxury.

Starting in Kampala, we've expanded our network to cover major business districts, educational institutions, and residential areas. Our hotspot technology ensures seamless connectivity whether you're working remotely, streaming entertainment, or staying connected with loved ones.
