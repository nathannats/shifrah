Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our service

How do I buy a voucher? ▼
Send payment to 0702 233 444 via MTN MoMo (*165#) or Airtel Money (*185#). Include your phone number in the transaction. You'll receive your voucher code via SMS instantly.
How long does my voucher last? ▼
Daily passes last 24 hours, weekly passes last 7 days, and monthly passes last 30 days. The timer starts when you first activate your code.
Can I use the same voucher on multiple devices? ▼
Monthly passes support up to 3 devices simultaneously. Daily and weekly passes are for single device use.
What happens after my voucher expires? ▼
You'll be disconnected from the network. Simply purchase a new voucher and activate it to continue browsing.
Is there a data limit? ▼
No! All our plans are truly unlimited. No data caps, no throttling, just fast internet for your entire session.
What areas do you cover? ▼
We currently cover Kampala City Center, Wandegeya, Nakasero, Ntinda, Makerere University, Najjera, and Bweyogerere. We're expanding to more areas every month!
